Main paper: component ablation (Experiment VI)

Average incremental accuracy on CIFAR-100 and TinyImageNet when toggling concept regularization and pseudo-concept generation. Skipping pseudo-concept generation leads to collapsed weights for previous classes; concept regularization alone does not prevent that. Combining both components yields the best results.

Appendix: additional ablations (summary)

The appendix analyzes concept generation, predictor design, robustness, distillation, VLM choice for concept activations, and qualitative behavior. Experiment indices follow the paper.

  • Experiment X — ConceptNet vs. GPT-3 for initial concept-set generation (Table: concept generator ablation).
  • Experiment XI — Sparsity in the final linear layer (dense vs. sparse SAGA-style training).
  • Experiment XII — Reduced concept availability (random masking of the concept set during training).
  • Experiment XIII — Gaussian noise injected into the concept-activation matrix at different SNRs.
  • Experiment XIV — Alternative strategies for learning the prediction layer (e.g., concept-space prototype variants).
  • Experiment XV — One-class-increment EFCIL regime.
  • Experiment XVI — Unique-concept expansion: number of concepts vs. number of seen classes.
  • Experiment XVII — Effect of distillation weight β on incremental accuracy.
  • Experiment XVIII — Impact of the distillation-based concept regularizer on concept fidelity (CLIP cosine similarity and Top-5 concept accuracy across phases).
  • Experiment XIX — CLIP vs. SigLIP for building the concept-activation matrix P (accuracy).
  • Experiment XX — Same VLM ablation with concept-fidelity metrics.
